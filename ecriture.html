Future-facing capabilities

More than a notation screen.

AI ensemble profiles

Planned tools for creating private ensemble profiles from authorized audio/video references to approximate a band's distinctive balance and sound. AI processing is not active in this demo.

Video and audio references

Planned reference upload and analysis workflows to help arrangers understand instrumentation, performance character, and the ensemble environment.

Team collaboration and protection

Planned director-staff collaboration, controlled sharing, and ownership-aware watermarking to discourage unauthorized file distribution.
