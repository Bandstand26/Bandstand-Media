WHY BANDSTAND MEDIA

Marching band culture deserves infrastructure built specifically for it.

Marching bands already create unforgettable performances, devoted fan communities and powerful cultural moments.

BandStand Media is building a digital ecosystem that helps that culture connect, create media, reach audiences and generate sustainable opportunity.

Instead of forcing marching band culture to fit platforms built for everyone else, BandStand is creating an environment centered around the people who make the culture move.
