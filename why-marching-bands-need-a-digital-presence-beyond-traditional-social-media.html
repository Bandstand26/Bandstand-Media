Why Marching Bands Need a Digital Presence Beyond Traditional Social Media

BandStand Media · November 28, 2026

General social networks are useful discovery channels, but a marching band should not depend entirely on a platform it cannot control. Algorithm changes, account restrictions and shifting audience habits can interrupt years of relationship-building.

Build an official home base

Maintain a school-approved website with current audition information, public events, contact details and authorized giving links. Ensure it works on mobile devices and is accessible without a social account. Assign responsibility for keeping information accurate.

Develop direct relationships

Invite alumni and supporters to opt into an institutional email list. Explain what updates they will receive and provide an unsubscribe mechanism. Store contact information securely and avoid purchasing lists or scraping personal data.

Create a durable archive

Keep authorized performance recordings, program history and media assets in approved storage. Document ownership and permissions. Social posts can point to the archive, but should not be the only copy of important institutional material.

Diversify distribution

Use short-form platforms for discovery, longer video for context, email for direct updates and specialized marching band communities for relevant connections. Evaluate each channel's privacy practices, costs and rights terms before adopting it.

Example: an integrated campaign

An approved performance clip directs viewers to an official event page. The page offers concert registration and an optional newsletter signup. After the concert, supporters receive a recap and information about future public events. Each channel has a clear role rather than duplicating every message.

Build resilience

Keep account recovery information with authorized staff, document publishing procedures and review links quarterly. Measure website inquiries, email engagement and event participation alongside social reach. The goal is a durable connection with the community, not dependence on any single platform.
